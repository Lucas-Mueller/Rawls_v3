## Simple Mode Instructions

**IMPORTANT: The stakes are much higher in this phase than in Phase 1.** This group decision will determine everyone's final earnings and has far greater consequences than your individual Phase 1 choices.

**GROUP DISCUSSION - Round {round_number} of {max_rounds}**

### Discussion History:
{discussion_history}

### The Four Justice Principles:
(a) **Maximizing the floor income**: Choose the distribution that maximizes the lowest income  
(b) **Maximizing the average income**: Choose the distribution that maximizes the average income  
(c) **Maximizing the average income with a floor constraint**: Maximize average while ensuring minimum income  
(d) **Maximizing the average income with a range constraint**: Maximize average while limiting income gap  

You are in the group discussion phase. Work with other participants to reach consensus on which justice principle the group should adopt.

### PREFERENCE-BASED CONSENSUS:
- Share your thoughts and reasoning about the principles with other participants
- Engage thoughtfully with other participants' viewpoints and reasoning
- When you are ready to commit to a principle, clearly state your preference
- End your statement with: "My preference is [principle choice]"
- For constraint principles (c or d), specify the constraint amount: "My preference is principle c with a floor constraint of $15,000"
- Consensus is reached when all participants state the same preference

### RESPONSE FORMAT:
Structure your discussion statement clearly:
1. Share your thoughts and reasoning about the principles
2. Engage with other participants' viewpoints
3. When ready to commit, clearly state: "My preference is [your choice]"

### EXAMPLES:
- "My preference is principle a" (maximizing floor income)
- "My preference is principle c with a floor constraint of $20,000"
- "My preference is maximizing the average income" (principle b)

The group's chosen principle will determine everyone's final earnings.  
If no consensus is reached, final earnings will be randomly determined.
